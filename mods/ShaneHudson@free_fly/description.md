Lets a party member that knows FLY carry you around the overworld. Take
off anywhere outdoors, fly over trees, water, fences and rooftops, cross
into neighbouring routes (the sea included), and press the B button over
open ground to land. You ride the Pokémon you picked, sized by its
Pokédex height.

Story gates and badges are respected by default, and everything is an
option: altitude, speed, badge checks, trainer sight, and a quick-start
Pidgey in Pallet Town that skips the long wait for HM02 (turn QUICK
START off for a vanilla start).

While airborne, doors, trainers, step events and ground battles wait
until you land; saving is blocked mid-air so you can never strand
yourself. Landing on water with a SURF knower puts you straight into
surfing.

Works in the flat 2D game and with the Dramatic Shape Voxel Mod (flight
clears rooftops in 3D, first and third person movement work, first
person gets a cockpit view). With wild_skies installed, flying into a
passing bird starts its battle mid-air.
