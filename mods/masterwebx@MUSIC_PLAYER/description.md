# Music Player

A modern in‑game music browser for Pokémon Gen 1 Recomp. Access the full soundtrack, mark favorites, and play custom queues without leaving the overworld.

## Features

### Quick Access
- **SELECT + A** opens the Music Player instantly from anywhere.
- **SELECT** while browsing restores the current map’s original music.

### Favorites System
- **START** marks or unmarks a track as a favorite.
- **SELECT + START** plays your favorites queue on loop.

### Smooth Integration
- Works seamlessly across overworld, menus, and battles.
- Respects map music, transitions, and scripted audio events.
- Fully compatible with custom tracks added by other mods.

## Controls Summary
- **SELECT + A** → Open Music Player  
- **START** → Toggle favorite  
- **SELECT + START** → Play favorites queue  
- **SELECT (in list)** → Restore map music  

Browse, favorite, and enjoy the soundtrack your way — all without breaking gameplay flow.
