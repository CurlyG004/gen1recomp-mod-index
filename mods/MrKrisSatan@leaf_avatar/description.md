# Choose Your Avatar

Adds a boy/girl question to Oak's intro and dresses the player in a female
avatar: walking and cycling sheets, fishing poses, battle back pic and front
pic, in four DMG shades so every COLORS mode including ADVANCED lights it.

Interoperates with the Dramatic Shape voxel renderer, and declares
`affects_link: false`: link play is unaffected.

## Install

1. Download `leaf_avatar-1.2.0.zip` from the
   [releases page](https://github.com/MrKrisSatan/Leaf/releases).
2. In the launcher: MODS → **Import mod .zip**.

With `"github": "MrKrisSatan/Leaf"` set, the launcher's **Update**
and **Versions** buttons handle new releases from there.
