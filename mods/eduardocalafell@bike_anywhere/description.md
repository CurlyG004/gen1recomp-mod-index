Ride the **BICYCLE anywhere** and hop on or off with a single key.

- **Ride anywhere** — no more "No cycling allowed here" and no getting kicked off the bike when you walk into a building, cave, or gym. Every map is bike-friendly.
- **Press `B` to mount / dismount** — skip the bag entirely. Tap `B` on the keyboard and you're on (or off) the bike right where you stand, sprite and music included.
- **Digital speedometer** — a cute green LCD readout in the bottom-right corner while you ride, revving up as you pedal and coasting back to 0 when you stop.

Everything respects the game's own rules: you can't get off on the Cycling Road, you can't bike while surfing, and by default you still need to own a BICYCLE to mount (toggleable). Each feature — ride anywhere, the `B` hotkey, requiring the bike, and the speedometer — has its own option, all ON by default.

Works in both the classic 2D view and the Dramatic Shape voxel view.
