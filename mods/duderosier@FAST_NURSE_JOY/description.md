# Fast Nurse Joy ⚡

Skip the wait at every Pokémon Center — and top everything up at the PC.

## ✨ Features

- **💬 No more dialogue** — talk to Nurse Joy and your Pokémon are healed
  on the spot. No "Would you like me to heal your Pokémon?", no YES/NO
  prompt, no "We hope to see you again!" The healing jingle still plays.
- **💻 PC healing** — open a PC and your whole party *and* every stored
  Pokémon are fully restored: HP, PP, and status conditions like poison,
  burn, sleep, paralysis and freeze all cleared.
- **🗺️ Works everywhere** — every Pokémon Center in the game.
- **🎯 Stays out of the way** — nothing else in the game is changed. Every
  other NPC, menu and script behaves exactly as normal.

## 📦 Install

1. Download the latest `.zip` from the [Releases](../../releases) page.
2. In Gen1Recomp, open the **Mods** panel and choose **Import mod .zip**.
3. Select the downloaded file and enable the mod.

## 🗑️ Uninstall

Disable or remove the mod in the **Mods** panel. Nurse Joy's dialogue and
the PC return to normal right away, and nothing is left behind in your
save file.

## 🎮 Compatibility

Built and tested against **Gen1Recomp v0.2.15+** with **Pokémon Gold**
(Gen 2).
