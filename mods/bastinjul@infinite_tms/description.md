# Infinite TMs

A small quality-of-life mod for [gen1recomp](https://github.com/bryanthaboi/gen1recomp): **TMs are never consumed when
you teach a move**. Teach Mega Punch, Seismic Toss, or any other TM to as many Pokémon as you want — the item stays in
your bag.

Currently supports **Gen 1** only. Gen 2 (Gold) support is planned for a future release.

## Features

- TMs are no longer removed from your inventory after a successful move-learning.
- HMs are unaffected (they were already reusable in vanilla Gen 1).
- Cancelling the learn prompt behaves exactly like vanilla — nothing changes there, since the TM was never consumed on
  cancel to begin with.
