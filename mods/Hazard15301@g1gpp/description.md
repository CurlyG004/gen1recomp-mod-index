# Gen 1 Glitch Preservation Project

G1GPP safely restores classic Pokémon Red and Blue glitch behavior in Gen1Recomp while containing destructive outcomes inside reversible, save-safe systems.

## Highlights

- Trainer-Fly and Ditto memory behavior with restored glitch Pokémon and bounded glitch-Trainer outcomes
- The Old Man coast glitch and MissingNo. sixth-item duplication
- Encounter-driven and Safari Zone save/reload Glitch City routes
- Pikablu/Marill and Bill's Secret Garden
- Cycling Road without a Bicycle
- A Pokémon Center PC SNAKE minigame
- Safe capture, party, Pokédex, save/load, and display handling for restored glitch species

## Compatibility

- Red and Blue are confirmed.
- Yellow is included but remains provisionally supported pending dedicated testing.
- Requires Gen1Recomp 0.2.36 through the compatible 0.2.x line.
- At least one of Gold, Silver, or Crystal must be imported. Enable G1GPP for that edition and launch it once to generate Marill graphics from the player's own imported cache. G1GPP gameplay remains inactive in Gen II.

No ROM-derived graphics, recorded game audio, ROMs, saves, or patches are distributed with the mod. Derived graphics are reconstructed at runtime from the player's own imported game caches.
